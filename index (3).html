A Second, Growing Problem

Black money re-entering the system through mule accounts

Beyond scam calls, a parallel problem is straining India's banking system: cash-intensive "black" money is layered through chains of mule accounts — often belonging to people who unknowingly rented or sold their bank credentials — until it re-emerges as clean "white" money. Banks respond by freezing large batches of suspect accounts, which too often catches genuine, unaware account holders in the same net.

RakshaAI's mule-network layer is built to replace blanket freezing with evidence: it scores accounts on pass-through behaviour, fan-in/fan-out patterns, account age, and holding time — the same signals RBI/RBIH's MuleHunter.AI initiative targets — so banks can act on real laundering conduits instead of collateral damage.
